# Open replicas

Jev itself is hosted, closed weights. The other half of launch-week buzz is: can I run this *shape* locally?

These imitate the interface (`state` + Choice/Score/Noul → probabilities), not TypeSafe’s weights. Independent. Not affiliated.

| Repo | Stars ~21 Sep | What it is |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [TheoLeeCJ/SemIf](https://github.com/TheoLeeCJ/SemIf) | ~2.9k | “Semantic ifs” from open models on a home 3090. |
| [jaredpalmer/kev](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev) | ~1.9k | Tiny Jev-like family on Qwen3.5 you train and run yourself. Earlier census had it ~582; it moved. |
| [TianyuCodings/NanoJev](https://github.com/TianyuCodings/NanoJev) | ~1.7k | Nano replica: parallel decisions, dynamic candidates, full training pipeline. Fastest-rising replica that week. |
| [vinnylarouge/jevlike](https://github.com/vinnylarouge/jevlike) | ~1.2k | Option-attention head over a changing candidate list. Research starter, not a Jev clone. Base for CUA-S1-FORMS. |
| [featherless-ai/simple-jev](https://github.com/featherless-ai/simple-jev) | ~437 | Wrap any open model as a `/systemone`-shaped classifier endpoint. |

Also in `repos/awesome-jev/categories/calibration-research.md`: Laya, openjev, LitJev, von, minojev, poorjev, openJev-verdict-2.0, jev-local.

Use these when you want a keyless stand-in or to study the interface. Do not treat their probabilities as TypeSafe Jev’s calibration.
